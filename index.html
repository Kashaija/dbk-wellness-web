Meet Danielle

Licensed Clinical Social Worker with a calm, directive style.

Danielle Kashaija is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker offering secure video therapy to adults, youth, couples, and families. Clients value her warm, collaborative approach and the way she pairs nervous-system education with practical skills for daily life.

  • Helps with anxiety, depression, trauma/PTSD, ADHD, family and relationship stress, women's issues.
  • Trauma-informed care with CBT, mindfulness, EMDR-informed resourcing, and attachment-focused work.
  • Telehealth-first: flexible scheduling for North Carolina clients and nearby regions.

Why this work matters

Evidence and experience in the same room.

PTSD touches 3.5% of U.S. adults each year, a 2024 survey found 26.8% of adults had been told they had depression, and about 6% of adults now carry an ADHD diagnosis. Danielle’s integrated, skills-forward approach addresses the overlap between these conditions so clients see relief faster and sustain it longer.

  • Structured first month: safety mapping, sleep stabilization, and coping toolkit.
  • Clear metrics: symptom check-ins and goal tracking at weeks 4, 8, and 12.
  • Collaborative care: coordination with PCP or psychiatry by request.
